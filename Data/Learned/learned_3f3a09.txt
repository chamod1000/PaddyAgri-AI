Query: How do I identify and control Brown Planthopper (BPH) pest attacks in paddy fields using DOA certified methods?
Verified Disease: Brown Planthopper (BPH) infestation
Symptoms: Yellowing or browning of leaves, Stunted growth, Presence of honeydew droplets on plants
Treatments: Synchronizing planting dates, Using resistant varieties, Introducing natural predators, Applying insecticides like buprofezin or dinotefuran
