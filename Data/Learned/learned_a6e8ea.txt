Query: What are the common symptoms of Paddy Blast disease and what chemical and organic treatments does the DOA recommend?
Verified Disease: Paddy Blast Disease (Pyricularia oryzae)
Symptoms: Small, circular, and sunken lesions on leaves, White or pinkish mycelium growth
Treatments: Apply recommended fungicide (e.g. Azoxystrobin), Use resistant varieties, Implement cultural methods such as draining water and keeping fields dry, Consider organic treatments like neem oil
