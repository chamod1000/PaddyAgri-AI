Query: What are the symptoms of Paddy Blast disease and how to control it?
Verified Disease: Paddy Blast disease
Symptoms: sudden appearance of small, circular, and sunken lesions on the leaves, white or pinkish mycelium growth, eventual death of the plant
Treatments: use of resistant varieties, cultural methods such as draining of water and keeping the fields dry, fertilization of the crop, application of fungicides such as azoxystrobin or pyraclostrobin at the right time
