Farmer Query: What are the common symptoms of Paddy Blast disease and what chemical and organic treatments does the DOA recommend?
Diagnosis: Paddy Blast Disease (Pyricularia oryzae)
Recommended Treatment: Apply recommended fungicide (e.g. Azoxystrobin), Use resistant varieties, Implement cultural methods such as draining water and keeping fields dry, Consider organic treatments like neem oil
Verified Advisory: ### 🔬 Disease Diagnosis Report
**Suspected Disease:** Paddy Blast Disease (Pyricularia oryzae)
**Confidence Level:** `High`

#### 🔍 Key Symptoms Identified:
- Small, circular, and sunken lesions on leaves
- White or pinkish mycelium growth

#### 🛡️ Recommended DOA Treatments:
- Apply recommended fungicide (e.g. Azoxystrobin)
- Use resistant varieties
- Implement cultural methods such as draining water and keeping fields dry
- Consider organic treatments like neem oil
