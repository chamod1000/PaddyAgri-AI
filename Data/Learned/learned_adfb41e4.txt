Diagnosis: Brown Planthopper (BPH) infestation
Recommended Treatment: Synchronizing planting dates, Using resistant varieties, Introducing natural predators, Applying insecticides like buprofezin or dinotefuran
Verified Advisory: ### 🔬 Disease Diagnosis Report
**Suspected Disease:** Brown Planthopper (BPH) infestation
**Confidence Level:** `High`

#### 🔍 Key Symptoms Identified:
- Yellowing or browning of leaves
- Stunted growth
- Presence of honeydew droplets on plants

#### 🛡️ Recommended DOA Treatments:
- Synchronizing planting dates
- Using resistant varieties
- Introducing natural predators
- Applying insecticides like buprofezin or dinotefuran
