# User Manual & Operating Instructions

## 1. Introduction

The **Multi-Agent Paddy AI Assistant** provides Sri Lankan paddy farmers, agricultural officers, and researchers with AI-driven disease diagnosis, NPK fertilizer calculation, weather risk advisories, and certified Department of Agriculture (DOA) guidelines.

---

## 2. Platform Navigation & Main Features

### 2.1 Starting a Diagnosis Query
1. Type your query in the chat input bar (e.g. *"What are the symptoms of Paddy Blast and how much Urea should I apply?"*).
2. Press **Enter** or click **Send**. The Multi-Agent Swarm will analyze your request and stream a structured report.

### 2.2 Uploading Paddy Leaf Photos
1. Click the **📎 Attachment Control** button near the input bar.
2. Select a paddy leaf photo (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`).
3. View the image preview card to confirm uploading.
4. Submit your query. The Vision Processing Layer will extract visible symptoms automatically.

### 2.3 Reviewing Structured Cards & Explainable AI (XAI)
- **Disease Subcard**: Displays suspected pathology, confidence tier, and DOA-approved treatments.
- **Fertilizer Subcard**: Displays Urea, TSP, and MOP per-acre dosages and split application schedules.
- **Safety Advisory**: Displays Pesticide Act No. 33 regulatory citations.
- **🔍 Why This Diagnosis?**: Expand this section to view visual lesion evidence, microclimate risks, and recommendation rationales.

### 2.4 Downloading PDF Crop Health Reports
After diagnosis completion, click **📄 Download Crop Health PDF Report** to download an official 10-section PDF summary.

### 2.5 Using Sidebar Features
- **🌾 Diagnosis History**: Review past cases, snapshots, and disease progression metrics.
- **📊 Platform Analytics**: View platform usage, top diagnosed diseases, and safety compliance metrics.
- **🌦 Weather & Seasonal Advisory**: View live humidity, temperature, fungal alert, and fertilizer timing guidance.
- **📚 Knowledge Center**: Search the DOA paddy reference library by keyword or category.
